bio_en: "Graphic designer in visual communication, educated at UNLP (Argentina). Self-taught visual artist and illustrator. Since 2015 she has lived in a coastal town, choosing to work on projects where environmental care, education and integration are the core pillars. Co-founder of \"Pulpart Mujeres en sus tintas\", Citrino Taller Gráfico and La Girada mini camping. Member of Yaqu Pacha Uruguay's Art and Environmental Education team, developing communication pieces aimed at children, tourism operators and the general community. \"Clara y las toninas\" is her first book collection fully illustrated by her, an initiative that has grown her love for the coast of Rocha and its marine wildlife. She always chooses to work in interdisciplinary teams because they are enriching experiences. She believes education through art can offer meaningful experiences that integrate new knowledge in a way that is sensitive and beneficial for our community."
