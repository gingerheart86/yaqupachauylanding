bio_en: "Actress, theatre teacher, puppet builder and teacher, artistic producer, yoga teacher, beekeeper. Trained in performing arts since 2001 at various institutions in Argentina and Uruguay. Actress and puppeteer in the environmental education pieces \"Toninas, Centinelas de la Costa\" and \"Ballena Franca Austral: La Gigante del Mar\".
