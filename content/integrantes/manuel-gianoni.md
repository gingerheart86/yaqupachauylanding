bio_en: "Bachelor's in Visual Arts, postgraduate degree in Artistic Education, Culture and Citizenship, diploma in Anthropology of Art. Artist, teacher and audiovisual producer. He is co-creator and coordinator of projects reflecting on territory, body, landscape and memory, among them \"Continuum: el cuerpo como territorio de prácticas mediales\" (2013, with Vivian Castro), \"Guion Abierto: Ensayos para la construcción de micronarrativas\" (2019, with Alejandra González Soca) and \"Cartografías Navegantes, en la Cuenca del río Negro\" (2022, with Ana Rodríguez). He has developed projects supported by national and international institutions, including MEC, MA, MGAP, MIEM, FFCC, DINAGUA, FRC, IMM, IMR, IDM, IMS, UDELAR, CURE, UNESCO, U. de Chile, MAM Chile, MACA, Fundación Kavlin and Telefónica."
